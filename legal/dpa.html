Legal & Privacy

Data Processing Agreement

Draft data processing agreement.

Draft version. To be reviewed before commercial launch.

This Data Processing Agreement is a draft for situations where Camp-Planner processes personal data on behalf of a customer or organisation.

Parties

The customer is the controller. Camp-Planner / TK Virksomhedsservice is the processor when the platform processes participant or event data on behalf of the customer.

Instructions

Camp-Planner processes personal data only on documented instructions from the customer unless legally required otherwise.

Purpose of processing

To provide the Camp-Planner platform, including event planning, participant management, instructor management, communication, check-in, live updates and support.

Categories of data subjects

Customer administrators, organisers, instructors, volunteers, participants and event-related contacts.

Categories of data

Name, contact details, organisation, role, registration, participant type, dietary/logistics details, accommodation preferences, check-in status, communication history and technical logs.

Security

Camp-Planner must apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access control, role-based access, backup, logging and trusted suppliers.

Subprocessors

Camp-Planner may use subprocessors such as GitHub, Vercel, Supabase and One.com. The list must be kept updated.

Deletion and return

On termination, Camp-Planner must delete or return personal data at the customer’s choice unless law requires continued retention.

Assistance

Camp-Planner must assist the customer with data subject rights, personal data breaches and documentation where relevant.
