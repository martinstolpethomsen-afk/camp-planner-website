Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Statistics and external forms have separate choices and are blocked until you allow the relevant category.

Necessary storage

Camp-Planner stores your external-content preference in your browser under cp-external-content-consent. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted or declined external services. Your separate statistics choice is stored under cp-analytics-consent. Existing consent for forms does not enable statistics. Advertising storage and personalization are disabled.

Change or withdraw your choice

New visitors see the cookie notice on every content page until they make a choice. You can reopen it using Cookie settings in the footer or the button below. Withdrawing statistics consent disables measurement, removes Google Analytics cookies for this website and reloads the page if Analytics was loaded. Withdrawing external-content consent reloads a page that has already loaded external content and prevents external content from loading on future page views; cookies already stored by a provider may need to be removed through your browser settings.
