Cookie Policy

Cookies and similar technologies.

Draft version. To be reviewed before commercial launch.

This Cookie Policy describes how Camp-Planner expects to use cookies and similar technologies on the website and platform.

Necessary cookies

Used to make the website, login, security and core functions work. These normally cannot be disabled.

Analytics and performance

May be used to understand website usage and improve the experience. These should only be activated after consent when not strictly necessary.

Third-party content

Embedded forms, booking tools or other third-party services may set cookies or similar technologies.

Consent

Before commercial launch, Camp-Planner should implement a cookie consent solution allowing users to accept, reject or change choices.

Changing cookie settings

Users can change cookie settings via the website once the consent solution is implemented, or through browser settings.
