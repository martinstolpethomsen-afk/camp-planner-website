This list describes current suppliers that may act as subprocessors or technical service providers for Camp-Planner.

GitHub

Purpose: source code, version control and static project files. Not intended for participant production data. Official DPA/privacy documentation should be archived before launch.

Vercel

Purpose: hosting and deployment of website and frontend. Vercel may process technical logs and frontend-related data.

Supabase

Purpose: database, authentication, backend services and application data. Supabase is central to the platform’s processing of event data.

One.com

Purpose: domain, DNS and domain-related services. One.com may process customer and domain-related information.

Future suppliers

Payment providers, email/SMS, analytics tools, support tools and print/badge services must be added before they are used in production.

Changes

Camp-Planner must notify relevant customers of material changes to subprocessors where required by agreement or law.
