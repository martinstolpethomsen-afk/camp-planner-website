Aufbewahrung & Sicherheit

Data retention, deletion and security overview.

Entwurf. Vor dem kommerziellen Launch rechtlich prüfen lassen.

This page describes Camp-Planner’s expected principles for data retention, deletion and security.

Marketing and demo requests

Kept as long as there is relevant dialogue, pilot interest or legitimate business reason. Inactive leads should be deleted or archived after a defined period.

Customer accounts

Kept while the customer relationship exists and for a reasonable period afterwards for support, documentation and accounting.

Event data

Should be kept while the event is active and for a defined period after the event. Afterwards, data should be deleted, anonymised or exported according to customer instruction.

Logs

Technical logs should have limited retention and only be used for security, debugging and operations.

Security measures

Role-based access, strong passwords or SSO where relevant, supplier security, backup, customer separation and limited access to production data.

Before launch

Camp-Planner should establish a concrete retention schedule, backup policy, incident response procedure, access review and documented supplier control.
