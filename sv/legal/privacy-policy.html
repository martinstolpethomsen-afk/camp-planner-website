Privacy and personal data processing.

Utkast. Ska granskas juridiskt före kommersiell lansering.

This Privacy Policy explains how Camp-Planner expects to process personal data in connection with the website, pilot programme, demos and the upcoming platform.

Controller

TK Virksomhedsservice, Solager 7, DK-2990 Nivaa, Denmark, Org. no. 42123242, is responsible for personal data collected through Camp-Planner’s own website, demo requests and own communication.

Data we may collect

Name, email, organisation, role, phone number, message content, demo and pilot interest, technical logs and information voluntarily submitted through forms.

Platform and event data

When Camp-Planner is used for events, the platform may process participant data, instructor data, schedule data, check-in status, accommodation preferences and communication history. For customer events, the customer will often be the controller and Camp-Planner the processor.

Purpose

Data is processed to provide the website, demos, pilot programme, support, event administration, communication, access control and operation of the platform.

Legal basis

Processing may be based on consent, contract, legitimate interest, legal obligation or processor instruction depending on the situation.

Retention

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose, support, documentation, contract relationship or legal obligations. Event data should be deleted or anonymised after a defined retention period.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, deletion, restriction, data portability and object to processing under GDPR.

Contact

Questions about privacy and data can be sent to Camp-Planner through the official contact channels on the website.
